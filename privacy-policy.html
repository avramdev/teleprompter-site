Privacy Policy

Teleprompter (com.avigram.teleprompter) is a local teleprompter and camera app from Avigram.

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or servers.

Camera, microphone, and Photos

With your permission, the app uses the front camera and microphone so you can preview and record yourself while you read. When you stop a recording, the video is saved only to Photos on your device. We cannot see those files.

You can use a script-only mode with no camera and no recording.

Scripts

Text you paste or type stays on your device and is not uploaded.

Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions: see Teleprompter Support.
