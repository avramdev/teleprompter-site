Camera, microphone, or Photos

Allow all three if you want to preview and record. You can also turn the camera off and use the script only. Change access later in Settings → Teleprompter.

Where is my video?

After you tap Stop, the clip is saved to Photos on this device. We never receive recordings or scripts.

It does not scroll or record

Unlock the phone, confirm permissions, then try Play or Rec again. In camera-off mode, Rec is hidden on purpose.
